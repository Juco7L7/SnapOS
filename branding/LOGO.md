# SnapOS logo

**The logo is a red snapping-turtle shell**: a hexagonal carapace of scutes
ringed with bold triangular spikes. The spikes symbolize **security**: SnapOS is
security-first, and the shell is armored.

**Snappy is not the logo.** Snappy is the small ASCII snapping turtle that
lives in the terminal (`snappy`, `snappy feed`, `snappy pet`) and in the
installer. The logo is the shell alone.

## Meaning

| Element | Symbolizes |
| --- | --- |
| Spiked shell | Security, hardened by default |
| Hex scutes | The declarative honeycomb: structured and self-contained |
| Red | Alert and decisive: snaps shut on threats |
